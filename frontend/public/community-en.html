Pismo Community Rules

This is an English translation provided for convenience. If anything in it differs from the Russian version at pismo.space/community.html, the Russian version prevails.

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Pismo is a messenger for personal communication, file sharing and calls. The rules below apply to everyone: in private chats and in groups. They exist to keep the service safe and are part of the Terms of Service.

1. What is allowed

  • personal communication, exchanging messages, files, photos/videos and voice messages;
  • group chats for friends, family, work or study;
  • audio and video calls;
  • criticising ideas, arguing, discussing any topic — politely and on the merits.

2. What is prohibited

  1. Threats, harassment, stalking. Intimidation, targeted harassment, incitement of hatred based on race, nationality, religion, sex, orientation or health.
  2. Sexual content involving minors — prohibited entirely, without exceptions. Such cases are reported to law enforcement.
  3. Violence and shock content. Images and videos of real violence, dismemberment, cruelty to people and animals.
  4. Fraud. Ponzi schemes, “prizes”, requests to transfer money under false pretexts, phishing links, selling goods that do not exist.
  5. Spam and unsolicited advertising. Bulk messaging, invitations from purchased phone number databases, pushing services.
  6. Malware and bypassing protection. Viruses, malicious files, programs for stealing passwords and data.
  7. Other people's personal data. Publishing someone's addresses, numbers, documents or chats without the owner's consent.
  8. Violating the law. Anything prohibited by the law of the Russian Federation, including materials recognised as extremist.
  9. Deceiving the service. Accounts created to bypass blocks, automated boosting, attempts to gain access to other people's accounts.

3. How to report

  • A message: long-press or the message menu → “Report”.
  • A person: the private chat header → “⋯” → “Report”.
  • Block the offender: in the same menu → “Block” (they will not be able to write, and the chat is hidden from the list).

A report goes to the moderator together with the context (message text, chat, time). Abusing reports (for example, mass-reporting good-faith users) is itself a violation.

4. What happens to offenders

  1. A message may be hidden automatically before review: if three different users reported it. The author then sees the “hidden until review” mark and can fix or delete the message.
  2. The moderator reviews the report and makes one of the decisions: “violation not confirmed” (the content is restored and the reporter receives an e-mail) or “violation confirmed” (the content stays hidden and the reporter receives an e-mail with the decision).
  3. Depending on the severity and repetition of violations: a warning, a restriction on sending messages, blocking the account, deleting the account.
  4. In cases of clear threats to life and health, and in cases involving minors, we contact law enforcement.

All actions are recorded in the moderation journal (who, when, what and why) — this is needed so that decisions can be traced.

5. Appeals

If you believe a moderator's decision is wrong, write to nyuskov@gmail.com: state the account phone number, describe the situation and attach a screenshot if it helps. We reply within 3 business days and review the decision if there are grounds for it.

6. Children

The Service is not intended for persons under 14. If it becomes known that an account is used by a child under 14, the account will be deleted.

7. Changes to the rules

The current version is always available at https://pismo.space/community-en.html. We report material changes in the app.

8. Contacts

Questions about the rules and moderation: nyuskov@gmail.com.
