Pismo Privacy Policy

This is an English translation provided for convenience. If anything in it differs from the Russian version at pismo.space/privacy.html, the Russian version prevails.

Last updated: 10 September 2026

1. Data controller

The personal data controller is an individual, Nyuskov Nikita Valeryevich. Contact for any requests about personal data processing (access, correction, blocking, deletion, withdrawal of consent): nyuskov@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

Pismo is a messenger. We process only the data without which it cannot work:

  • Account data: phone number (login), confirmation code, first and last name, username, e-mail (if provided), date of birth, country, language, status, avatar.
  • Chat content: text messages, attachments (images, videos, voice messages, files), reactions, service data (time, author, read receipts). Chats with end-to-end encryption (E2EE) are stored on the server only in encrypted form — the keys are on users' devices, and we cannot read such messages.
  • At your request: choosing a person from contacts, searching users by name or number.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, app version, request logs, push subscription identifier, crash data.
  • Camera and microphone: used only during an audio/video call and when recording voice messages. The call media stream goes directly between participants (WebRTC).

We do not process biometric data, do not collect geolocation, do not use data for advertising and do not share it with third parties for marketing.

3. Why we process data (purposes)

All processing purposes relate to a single service — messaging via Pismo:

  • registration and sign-in (verifying the phone number);
  • delivery and storage of messages, files and media;
  • notifications about new messages and incoming calls (push);
  • security: protection from spam, code brute-forcing and unauthorised access, rate limiting, warnings about a sign-in from a new device and about password/e-mail changes;
  • user support and fixing technical failures.

4. Legal grounds

Processing is carried out on the basis of the user's consent (Article 6 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ “On Personal Data”), given at registration, and for the purpose of performing the agreement on the use of the service — Terms of Service, which the user concludes at registration.

5. Storage and protection

  • data is stored on servers located in the territory of the Russian Federation;
  • data transfer between the app and the server is protected with TLS/HTTPS encryption;
  • access to data is limited to the people maintaining the service;
  • for certain chats end-to-end encryption (E2EE) is available: the content of such messages is not available to the server;
  • storage period — as long as the account exists; after account deletion the data is deleted, and backups — within 30 days.

6. Sharing with third parties

Data is not sold and is not shared with third parties, except when it is necessary for the service to work (for example, delivering push notifications via a push service operator) or required by law.

Transfer outside the Russian Federation (cross-border transfer). Notifications about new messages in the browser are delivered through browser vendors' push services: Google (Chrome, Edge, Opera — USA), Apple (Safari), Mozilla (Firefox). For delivery abroad we transfer the push subscription identifier of your device (delivery service address and encryption keys), the sender's name and the notification text (no more than 120 characters). Push notifications can be turned off in the profile — then such transfer stops. Notifications for the Android app are delivered via the RuStore service (Russia). All other data, including chats and attachments, is processed and stored on servers located in the Russian Federation and is not transferred abroad.

7. Your rights

  • to obtain information about the processing of your personal data;
  • to correct data in the app profile;
  • to withdraw consent and delete the account — by writing to nyuskov@gmail.com;
  • to appeal the controller's actions to Roskomnadzor or to a court.

8. Children

The Service is not intended for persons under 14. We do not knowingly collect children's data; if such data is discovered, it will be deleted.

9. Changes to the policy

The current version is always available at https://pismo.space/privacy-en.html. We notify users about material changes in the app.

10. Contacts

For any questions about personal data processing: nyuskov@gmail.com.
